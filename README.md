# ESG Value Extractor

An NLP-based tool for extracting and identifying ESG-related information from corporate reports and sustainability disclosures.

## Overview

ESG Value Extractor is a Python-based project designed to help identify relevant Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) information from unstructured corporate documents.

The project focuses on extracting meaningful ESG keywords, terms, and values from company disclosures, making it easier to analyze sustainability-related information.

## Key Features

- Extract ESG-related keywords from text
- Identify relevant sustainability information
- Process unstructured corporate disclosures
- Organize extracted information for further analysis
- Python-based NLP workflow
- Modular project structure for future development

## ESG Categories

The project is designed around three major ESG dimensions:

### Environmental
Examples include:

- Carbon emissions
- Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 emissions
- Renewable energy
- Energy consumption
- Water usage
- Waste management
- Biodiversity
- Climate change

### Social
Examples include:

- Employee welfare
- Diversity and inclusion
- Health and safety
- Human rights
- Community development
- Training and development

### Governance
Examples include:

- Board governance
- Business ethics
- Anti-corruption
- Risk management
- Data privacy
- Corporate governance
- Regulatory compliance
